planted fault #3 Horizontal overflow on mobile

The panel below has a fixed width of 720px and is not wrapped in a scrollable container. On a desktop viewport (1280px) it fits; on a typical phone viewport (390px) it forces the whole document to scroll sideways. ReproCase measures scrollWidth vs clientWidth at both viewports and locates the offending elements.
